    Questions people ask first

    What's the shortest stay?

    One night. Nightly rates drop at 7, 30 and 90 nights, and the calculator at the top shows exactly where.

    Are electricity and internet included?

    Yes, up to a fair-use limit of 300 units of electricity a month on one-bedrooms and 450 on two-bedrooms. Internet is unlimited.

    Can I check in late at night?

    Any time. The desk is staffed around the clock, and we track your flight if you send us the number.

    Do you need a deposit?

    For personal stays over 30 nights, one month's rent, refunded within 7 days of checkout. Company accounts don't pay deposits.

    Can I bring a pet?

    Small dogs and cats are welcome at Whitefield and Hebbal, with a one-time cleaning fee of ₹3,000.
